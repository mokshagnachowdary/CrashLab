# Model card — CRASHLAB scenario generators
**Intended use:** research/education stress testing; exploring *conditional, model-based* multi-asset scenarios. **Not** for trading, price prediction, regulatory capital or advice.
**Models:** Gaussian; Gaussian copula & Student-t copula (t marginals); GJR-GARCH(1,1)-t Filtered Historical Simulation; conditional GRU-VAE (PyTorch, CPU-friendly, ~tens of thousands of parameters).
**cVAE inputs:** per-asset cumulative-return targets + masks, horizon (≤20 trading days), vol regime. **Outputs:** daily multivariate log-return paths. Runtime conditioning draws native CVAE proposals, then uses correlation-aware Gaussian-kernel weights to select intact paths; unconditional baselines use the same selection over unconditional proposals. No path is rescaled or rewritten. Targets are probabilistic, not hard constraints; the report includes predictive intervals, sampling uncertainty, proposal support, and violations.
**Training data:** daily log returns of NIFTY 50/Bank/IT, gold & crude futures, USD/INR; chronological 60/20/20 split; scalers and regime thresholds fit on train only. Provenance is recorded in `artifacts/manifest.json` (`synthetic: false` = Yahoo Finance history, unofficial, subject to provider terms; the pipeline refuses to fall back to synthetic data with `--no-synthetic`).
**Evaluation:** see `docs/RESEARCH_REPORT.md` (generated from artifacts).
**Known limitations:** finite proposal pools can provide weak support for extreme targets; kernel selection changes the proposal distribution and its effective sample size is reported; unprecedented regimes cannot be validated; the cVAE needs 5–20-day windows; copulas have no volatility dynamics; futures proxies; small backtest samples; single-seed ablations; FHS is volatility-state-conditioned while the other generators are not; baseline fits use train+validation while CVAE weights use train only, so training sample sizes differ.
